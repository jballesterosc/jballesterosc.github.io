Umbral is a civic technology and open-data lab I run, building reproducible monitoring products and tools for Mexico — for both people and, increasingly, AI agents. Code ships under MIT and datasets under CC BY 4.0.

The four projects below (DesaparecidosMX, Observatorio Delictivo, Política Meta, and Cabildo Libre) are all part of Umbral.
